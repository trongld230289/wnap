# YNAB Pro Tips: Mastering the Filter Cards

To help you operate these buttons like a financial pro, let's dive into real-world **Use Cases**. Think of these cards as your "Information Assistants" that prevent you from feeling overwhelmed by dozens of spending categories.

---

## 1. Card: Overspent
**The Situation:** You went out for sushi and spent $100, but your "Dining Out" jar only had $50. YNAB is now showing a Red/Yellow warning.
* **How to use:** Click this card to isolate only the categories with negative balances.
* **The Benefit:** These are your "House on Fire" categories. This filter saves you from hunting through your entire budget to find where the leak is.
* **Action:** Move $50 from your "Shopping" jar to cover the hit. Done! Your budget is beautiful and green again.

## 2. Card: Underfunded
**The Situation:** It’s the 1st of the month and your paycheck just arrived. You have 20 categories (Electricity, Rent, Netflix...) that are all empty and glowing Yellow because they haven't met their Targets yet.
* **How to use:** Click this to see everything that requires funding this month.
* **The Benefit:** Massive time saver. Instead of manual entry, select this card and hit **Auto-Assign**. YNAB pours money into these priority jars until they hit their target lines.
* **Action:** Fund your most critical goals first.

## 3. Card: Overfunded
**The Situation:** You set a goal to save $500 for a new phone. However, you accidentally assigned $600, or perhaps you had a leftover balance from last month that you didn't notice.
* **How to use:** Click this to find the jars that are "too rich."
* **The Benefit:** Optimizes your cash flow. Money sitting idly without a job is "lazy money." This card helps you find those surpluses so you can put them to better use.
* **Action:** Move the extra funds into an investment fund or use them to pay down debt.

## 4. Card: Money Available
**The Situation:** You’re standing at the mall and want to know: *"What can I actually spend money on right now without hurting my budget?"*
* **How to use:** Click this to filter out all categories that are empty or have a zero balance.
* **The Benefit:** Helps you focus on what you actually have. It removes the "noise" of unfunded categories, giving you the confidence to spend without second-guessing.
* **Action:** See that the "Entertainment" jar has $20 left -> Go ahead and buy that movie ticket!
