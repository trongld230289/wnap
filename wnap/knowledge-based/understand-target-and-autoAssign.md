# YNAB Guide: Understanding Targets and Auto-Assign

To understand the inspector panel on the right, imagine your budget categories are **empty jars**.

## 1. What is a "Target"?
A **Target** is like taking a marker and drawing a horizontal line on the jar, writing: *"I must fill the money up to this line to have enough."*

* **Why do you need it?** Without a Target, YNAB doesn't know how much money you actually need. Once you set a Target (e.g., $300 for Insurance), YNAB looks at your current balance. If you have $0, it turns **Yellow (Underfunded)** to warn you.
* **In your screenshot:** You have the "Insurance" category selected, but you haven't set a target yet (the "Create Target" button is still visible). If you click that and set it to $300/month, YNAB will help you track whether you've "poured" enough money in for the month.

## 2. What is "Auto-Assign"?
Think of this as an **"Automatic Money Dispenser."** Instead of manually typing numbers into the "Assigned" column, you use these buttons to let YNAB do the work for you.

Looking at the options in your panel:
* **Underfunded:** The machine prioritizes filling jars that haven't reached the "marker line" (Target) you set.
* **Assigned Last Month:** *"Whatever I put in this jar last month, do the exact same this month."*
* **Spent Last Month:** *"However much I spent last month, refill it by that exact amount to spend again."*
* **Average Spent:** The machine calculates your average spending over the past few months and fills the jar accordingly.

## 3. How do I know which category to fund first?
When you have **$1,320.00** sitting in **Ready to Assign (RTA)**, don't just assign it randomly. Follow this "survival" priority order:

### Step 1: Fix NEGATIVE balances (Red)
In your image, your "TV, phone and internet" category is at **-$200.00 (Overspent)**.
* **Priority #1:** You must use your RTA money to cover this immediately. In YNAB, a red balance is a "fire" that needs to be put out right away.

### Step 2: Bills with Due Dates
Look at your **Bills** group. Which ones are due before your next paycheck?
* If the electricity bill is due on the 15th and today is the 10th -> **Fund it now.**
* If Insurance isn't due until next month -> **You can wait.**

### Step 3: Essential Needs
Groceries, Transportation, etc. You can't go hungry while waiting for your next check. Estimate how much you need for food until you get paid again, and fill those jars.

### Step 4: True Expenses and Savings
Once your stomach is full and bills are paid, start pouring money into jars like Car Repairs, Gifts, or Vacation goals.

## Pro Tips for You:
* **Smart Filters:** Instead of selecting categories one by one, try clicking the **Underfunded** filter card at the top. YNAB will filter out every category where you "promised" to add money (via a Target) but haven't yet.
* **Actionable Advice:** You have a significant amount in RTA ($1,320). Your very first move should be clicking that **-$200.00** in the TV category to fix it, then use **Auto-Assign** for the rest!
