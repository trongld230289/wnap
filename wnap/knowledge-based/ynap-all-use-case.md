# 📘 YNAB FAMILY HANDBOOK

## PHASE 1: SETTING UP YOUR FINANCIAL "MAP"

### Situation 1: Designing Your "Money Jars" (Categories)
* **Action:** You and your spouse sit down and create **Category Groups** to organize priorities.
* **Suggested Structure:**
    * **Fixed Bills:** Rent/Mortgage, Utilities, Internet, Insurance.
    * **Daily Needs:** Groceries, Transportation, Baby supplies (diapers/milk).
    * **True Expenses:** Car repairs, Medical, Gifts (Large but infrequent expenses).
    * **Personal/Fun:** "Freedom" funds for both husband and wife to spend individually.

### Situation 2: Connecting Accounts
* **Action:** Add all bank accounts, e-wallets, cash, and **Credit Cards**.
* **Note:** For credit cards, YNAB automatically holds money from your spending categories to ensure you always have enough to pay off the card balance.

---

## PHASE 2: ASSIGNING CASH FLOW

### Situation 3: Payday - Give Every Dollar a Job (Rule 1)
* **The Signal:** The **Ready to Assign (RTA)** number turns green.
* **Action:**
    1. Ask yourselves: *"What does this money need to do until the next paycheck arrives?"*
    2. Fill the **Assigned** column in this priority: **Red (Overspent) > Upcoming Bills > Groceries/Gas.**
    3. Bring the **RTA number down to zero**.

### Situation 4: Automating Your Plan with "Auto-Assign"
To make funding faster and more accurate, use the **Auto-Assign** panel on the right side of the screen:

| Button | How it Works (Mechanism) | When to Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Underfunded** | Subtracts current balance from your **Target** and fills the gap. | **Bills & Savings:** Rent, Internet, Insurance. |
| **Assigned Last Month** | Copies the exact amount you typed in the Assigned column last month. | **Fixed Allowances:** Pocket money, money sent to parents. |
| **Spent Last Month** | Assigns exactly what you actually spent last month. | **Variable Spending:** Groceries, Dining out, Coffee. |
| **Average Assigned** | Calculates the average amount assigned over the last 12 months. | **Long-term Reserves:** Home repairs, electronics upgrades. |
| **Average Spent** | Calculates the average of your actual monthly spending. | **Seasonal Bills:** Electricity and water. |
| **Reset Available** | Empties the Available column and moves the money back to **RTA**. | **Month-end Cleanup:** To gather extra cash for debt/investing. |
| **Reset Assigned** | Resets the Assigned column for the current month back to zero. | **Quick Correction:** When you want to restart your plan for the month. |

## PHASE 3: DAILY ACTION & FLEXIBILITY

### Situation 5: Just Spent Money (Recording Transactions)
* **Action:** Enter transactions immediately at the checkout counter.
* **Understand Your Balances:**
    * **Uncleared Balance:** Spent money that hasn't been finalized by the bank (indicated by a gray **C**).
    * **Working Balance:** Your true remaining balance (includes all pending transactions).

### Situation 6: When You Overspend (Rule 3 - Roll With The Punches)
* **Using the "Overspent" Card:** Filter for categories showing **Red** or **Yellow**.
* **Action:** Click the negative number and choose to "cover" the overspending using funds from an **Overfunded** category.

---

## PHASE 4: MAINTENANCE & RECONCILIATION

### Situation 7: Checking with the Bank (Reconcile)
* **Frequency:** Every 2-3 days.
* **Action:** Compare your bank app's balance with the **Cleared Balance** in YNAB -> Click **Reconcile**.
* **The Benefit:** Ensures your data is the "truth," giving you confidence before making big purchases.

---

## PHASE 5: FAMILY SYNCING

### Situation 8: Using YNAB Together
* **Action:** Invite your spouse via the **YNAB Together** feature in Settings to share one budget across two separate devices.

### Situation 9: Check Your Wallet Before You Spend
* **Using "Money Available":** Before shopping, click this card to see which "jars" have green money available and stay within that limit.

---

### 💡 Quick Color Reference:
* 🔴 **Red:** "House on Fire" (Cover the overspending immediately).
* 🟡 **Yellow:** Underfunded relative to Target (Needs more money).
* 🟢 **Green:** Ready to spend.
